How to play

  • Trace words of 4+ letters by dragging along the paths that connect letters — paths can run diagonally too. Only connected letters can follow each other.
  • Every path on the board belongs to at least one hidden word — the word list on the side shows how many of each length remain. ★ marks the longest word in the puzzle.
  • The arc around each letter shows its remaining uses: one segment per unfound word, disappearing as you find them.
  • When a letter's last word is found, it turns into a frog. Paths disappear once every word using them is found.
  • Tap groups of 2+ same-colored frogs to pop them for bonus points (bigger groups score much more).
  • Every common word you can trace is part of the puzzle — and finding a plural clears its singular too. Rarer words earn a small bonus.
  • Find every word to clear the pond! Every puzzle is generated on the fly and is always solvable.
